---
description: Everything that wants a decision this morning, worst first. Missed close dates, deals with no next step, stalled and single-threaded deals, untouched leads, quota gaps, quiet customers.
---

1. Run `npm run crm -- attention`.
2. The list is already ordered by how much each item can cost. Read it in that order and do not reorder it by ease:
   - **A close date missed with the deal still open** outranks everything: the quarter's number is wrong until it is fixed. Move the date or close the deal.
   - **Closing inside fourteen days with no next step** is a deal about to slip in silence.
   - **A next step overdue** is a promise the rep made to the pipeline and broke.
   - **A quota gap** is a quarter that needs deals pulled forward or expectations reset now, not at review.
   - **A stalled deal** (three weeks silent) needs one honest touch or an honest `opp lost`.
   - **A six-figure deal on one relationship** dies when that one person changes jobs. Name the second contact to engage.
   - **An untouched lead** is conversion decaying by the day.
   - **A quiet customer** is next year's churn being quietly decided.
3. For each item, say the one action: the command to run, the call to make, or the draft to write. Name the rep who owns it.
4. Anything that needs an email is drafted to `drafts/`, never sent.

If the operator asks "what should I do today", pick the top three and say why those three.
